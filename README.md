# Telco Customer Churn Analysis | Python + SQL

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by telecom companies, directly impacting revenue, profitability, and customer lifetime value.

To better understand the factors driving customer attrition, I completed an end-to-end Customer Churn Analysis project using Python and SQL.

## Project Objectives

* Measure overall churn rate
* Identify key churn drivers
* Segment high-risk customers
* Estimate revenue impact of churn
* Generate actionable business recommendations

## Dataset

* Source: Kaggle Telco Customer Churn Dataset
* Total Customers: 7,043
* Churned Customers: 1,869
* Churn Rate: 26.54%

## Key Findings

### Contract Type Matters

Customers on month-to-month contracts exhibited the highest churn rates, while long-term contracts showed significantly better retention.

### Early Lifecycle Customers Are Most Vulnerable

Customers with less than 12 months of tenure were far more likely to leave compared to long-term customers.

### High Charges Increase Churn Risk

Customers paying higher monthly charges demonstrated a greater likelihood of churning.

### Support Services Improve Retention

Customers without Tech Support services churned at substantially higher rates.

### Payment Method Influences Behavior

Electronic check users showed higher churn rates, whereas customers using automatic payment methods were more likely to stay.

## High-Risk Customer Segment

The highest churn probability was observed among customers who:

* Had month-to-month contracts
* Had tenure below 12 months
* Paid more than $70 per month

This segment presents a strong opportunity for targeted retention campaigns.

## Business Impact

Estimated monthly revenue loss due to churn:

**$139,130**

This highlights the importance of proactive customer retention strategies and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Recommendations

* Encourage migration to long-term contracts through incentives
* Strengthen onboarding during the first 6–12 months
* Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customers
* Promote value-added services such as Tech Support and Security
* Encourage adoption of automatic payment methods
